SMS Terms

Program name

Monsey Clothing Sales alerts

What it is

Text messages from Monsey Clothing Sales to parents who signed up: announcements of upcoming children's clothing sales (dates, times and what's on sale), registration updates, and reminders about the shopping time slot you reserved.

How to join

Enter your mobile phone number on our registration form (linked from monseyclothingsales.com) and check the box agreeing to receive text messages from Monsey Clothing Sales. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How often

Message frequency varies, usually a few messages per sale.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Opt out and help

Reply STOP at any time to cancel. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed and nothing after that. To join again, reply START or register again on our website.

Reply HELP for help, or just reply to any of our texts with your question. You can also email info@monseyclothingsales.com.
