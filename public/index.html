Text message alerts (SMS) — details

How to sign up for texts: on the registration form (Boys button above), enter your mobile phone number and check the box agreeing to receive text messages from Monsey Clothing Sales.

By providing your mobile number and agreeing on the registration form, you consent to receive recurring text messages from Monsey Clothing Sales about sale announcements, registration updates, and time-slot reminders.

  • Message frequency varies (usually a few messages per sale).
  • Msg & data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP to opt out at any time. Reply HELP for help.
  • Consent is not a condition of purchase.
  • Your mobile number and text opt-in data are never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.
