Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Monsey Clothing Sales runs a sign-up list so parents can hear about our upcoming children's clothing sales. This page explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • How many children you have, their ages and their school
  • If you choose to give them on the web form: your email and home address

How we use it

Only to run our sales: announcing upcoming sales, registration updates, and reminders about the shopping time slot you reserved, by text message (and by email if you gave one). We do not use it for anything else.

We never sell or share it

We never sell, rent or share your information with third parties.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Text messages

You join our text list by entering your mobile number on our registration form (linked from monseyclothingsales.com) and agreeing to receive text messages from Monsey Clothing Sales. Messages cover sale announcements, registration updates and time-slot reminders. Message frequency varies (a few per sale). Msg & data rates may apply. Consent is not a condition of purchase.

How to opt out

Reply STOP to any of our texts at any time and you will get no more messages. Reply HELP for help.

Contact us

Questions, or want your information removed? Just reply to any of our text messages, or email info@monseyclothingsales.com, and we'll take care of it.
